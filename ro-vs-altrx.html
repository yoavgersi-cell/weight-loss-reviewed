Common questions

Is Ro better than AltRx?

In our scoring Ro comes out ahead — 9.1/10 to 7.4/10, leading on transparency. It's our pick for most people; AltRx is the better choice if you want the lowest possible entry cost.

Which is cheaper, Ro or AltRx?

Ro has the lower advertised starting price — $39/mo vs $89/mo for AltRx. But read the fine print: Ro is membership + medication (billed separately) and AltRx is all-in, includes medication, and the cheapest rates often need a prepaid multi-month plan — so compare the real all-in cost at your maintenance dose, not the headline.

Do Ro and AltRx prescribe the same medications?

Ro offers wegovy, zepbound, ozempic & foundaya (oral pill or injection pen); AltRx offers compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide (weekly injection). On breadth of options, Ro scores higher. Compounded GLP-1s aren't FDA-approved finished products, and availability shifts — confirm current options with each provider.

Which gives you more support, Ro or AltRx?

Ro scores higher on support — real clinician access, coaching and follow-up. For visits, Ro is async + optional video and AltRx is async, video when needed.

Are Ro and AltRx legit?

Both require a licensed clinician to review your intake before prescribing. Confirm each provider's credentials, state availability (Ro: nationwide; AltRx: not stated) and current pricing before you sign up — and note that compounded medications aren't FDA-approved.

Can I switch between Ro and AltRx?

Generally yes — neither locks you into a long contract, though the lowest prices often require a prepaid plan. Never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
