WellMedr FAQ

How much does WellMedr really cost per month?

At the dose most people stay on, expect about $129/mo — all-in, includes medication. The advertised starting rate is $49/mo, but that's usually an intro or prepay figure. Lowest rate on 12-month prepay; month-to-month advertised at $88/mo; tirzepatide from ~$89/mo. Confirm the current price at exploretreatments.com.

What medications does WellMedr prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with WellMedr.

Does WellMedr take insurance?

WellMedr is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do WellMedr visits work?

Async intake + video/messaging. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is WellMedr available?

All 50 states. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is WellMedr legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
