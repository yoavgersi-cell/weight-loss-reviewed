How bmiMD works

Getting started with bmiMD follows the standard telehealth path: you fill in an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it (telehealth consult + unlimited messaging), and if a GLP-1 is appropriate, semaglutide & tirzepatide is prescribed and shipped to your door. It includes medication, supplies, temp-controlled shipping, physician monitoring.

  1. Medical intake — a short online questionnaire about your health, history and goals.
  2. Clinician review — telehealth consult + unlimited messaging; a licensed prescriber decides whether a GLP-1 is safe and appropriate for you.
  3. Medication & delivery — compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide ships to your home, with ongoing support: medication, supplies, temp-controlled shipping, physician monitoring.

bmiMD FAQ

How much does bmiMD cost?

bmiMD starts at $99/mo — all-in, includes medication. On 12-month plan; $109 (6-mo) / $119 (3-mo) / $129–$159 month-to-month. Confirm the current price at glp1picks.com.

What medications does bmiMD prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with bmiMD.

Does bmiMD take insurance?

bmiMD is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do bmiMD visits work?

Telehealth consult + unlimited messaging. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is bmiMD available?

48 states (excl. LA, MS). Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is bmiMD legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
