How HealthRx works

Getting started with HealthRx follows the standard telehealth path: you fill in an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it (async health assessment), and if a GLP-1 is appropriate, semaglutide & tirzepatide is prescribed and shipped to your door. It includes medication, overnight shipping, money-back guarantee.

  1. Medical intake — a short online questionnaire about your health, history and goals.
  2. Clinician review — async health assessment; a licensed prescriber decides whether a GLP-1 is safe and appropriate for you.
  3. Medication & delivery — compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide ships to your home, with ongoing support: medication, overnight shipping, money-back guarantee.

HealthRx FAQ

How much does HealthRx cost?

HealthRx starts at $99/mo — all-in, includes medication. On 12-month plan; $133/mo (3-month) to $189/mo month-to-month; same price at every dose. Confirm the current price at matchglp1.com.

What medications does HealthRx prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with HealthRx.

Does HealthRx take insurance?

HealthRx is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do HealthRx visits work?

Async health assessment. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is HealthRx available?

Not clearly stated. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is HealthRx legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
