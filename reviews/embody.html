embody FAQ

How much does embody cost?

embody starts at $69/mo — all-in, includes medication. Promo starting rate; ~$299/mo at standard/maintenance dosing. Confirm the current price at exploretreatments.com.

What medications does embody prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection or daily oral. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with embody.

Does embody take insurance?

embody is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do embody visits work?

Clinician-reviewed telehealth. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is embody available?

Not stated. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is embody legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
