Is Shed legit?

Yes — Shed is a legitimate US-based telehealth weight-loss provider. In practice, it works with US compounding pharmacies, and a licensed clinician reviews your intake before prescribing.

  • A licensed clinician reviews your intake before any prescription

  • Medication form: injection, drops or lozenge
  • Available: all 50 states
  • Cash-pay (HSA/FSA accepted) — no insurance needed

"Legitimate" means a real, licensed telehealth operation — not a guarantee of results. Compounded medications are not FDA-approved brand drugs. Always confirm current details and eligibility with the provider.

Shed FAQ

How much does Shed really cost per month?

At the dose most people stay on, expect about $199/mo — all-in, includes medication. The advertised starting rate is $149/mo, but that's usually an intro or prepay figure. Intro/platform rate; semaglutide injection commonly from $199/mo, dose-tiered; ~2-month minimum. Confirm the current price at glpchart.com.

What medications does Shed prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as injection, drops or lozenge. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with Shed.

Does Shed take insurance?

Shed is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do Shed visits work?

Async questionnaire. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is Shed available?

All 50 states. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is Shed legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
