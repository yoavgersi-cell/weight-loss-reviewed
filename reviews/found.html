Is Found legit?

Yes — Found is a legitimate US-based telehealth weight-loss provider. In practice, it works with US compounding pharmacies, and it can prescribe FDA-approved branded medication, and a licensed clinician reviews your intake before prescribing.

  • A licensed clinician reviews your intake before any prescription

  • Access to FDA-approved branded medication (Wegovy, Zepbound)

Found FAQ

How much does Found really cost per month?

At the dose most people stay on, expect about $199/mo — membership + medication (billed separately). The advertised starting rate is $198/mo, but that's usually an intro or prepay figure. $99–$149/mo Rx membership + compounded from $99/mo (or $49/mo coaching-only, no Rx). Confirm the current price at joinfound.com.

What medications does Found prescribe?

Compounded + branded (Wegovy, Zepbound), taken as injection or oral. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with Found.

Does Found take insurance?

Found runs an insurance concierge that pursues coverage for branded medication; many members also pay cash.

How do Found visits work?

Video (insurance) or async (self-pay). You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is Found available?

Nationwide. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is Found legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
