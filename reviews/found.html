How Found works

Getting started with Found follows the standard telehealth path: you fill in an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it (video (insurance) or async (self-pay)), and if a GLP-1 is appropriate, wegovy, zepbound is prescribed and shipped to your door. It includes clinician care, behavioral coaching, app, insurance navigation.

  1. Medical intake — a short online questionnaire about your health, history and goals.
  2. Clinician review — video (insurance) or async (self-pay); a licensed prescriber decides whether a GLP-1 is safe and appropriate for you.
  3. Medication & delivery — compounded + branded (wegovy, zepbound) ships to your home, with ongoing support: clinician care, behavioral coaching, app, insurance navigation.

Found FAQ

How much does Found cost?

Found starts at $198/mo — membership + medication (billed separately). $99–$149/mo Rx membership + compounded from $99/mo (or $49/mo coaching-only, no Rx). Confirm the current price at joinfound.com.

What medications does Found prescribe?

Compounded + branded (Wegovy, Zepbound), taken as injection or oral. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with Found.

Does Found take insurance?

Found runs an insurance concierge that pursues coverage for branded medication; many members also pay cash.

How do Found visits work?

Video (insurance) or async (self-pay). You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is Found available?

Nationwide. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is Found legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
