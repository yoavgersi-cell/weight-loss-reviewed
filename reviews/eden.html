The bottom line

Eden's pitch is refreshingly simple: one flat price, the same every month no matter your dose, with no membership and nothing to cancel your way out of. For people who hate watching a GLP-1 bill creep up as they titrate, that predictability is worth a lot. It's compounded-only and the coaching is light, so it suits a self-directed buyer who wants a clean price and a human to reach — not a hand-holding program.

Eden FAQ

How much does Eden really cost per month?

At the dose most people stay on, expect about $99/mo — all-in flat price, includes medication. The advertised starting rate is $99/mo, but that's usually an intro or prepay figure. Flat $99/mo for compounded semaglutide and $129/mo for compounded tirzepatide — the same price at every dose, with no membership fee or long-term plan required. Confirm the current price at tryeden.com.

What medications does Eden prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with Eden.

Does Eden take insurance?

Eden is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do Eden visits work?

Doctor-guided telehealth. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is Eden available?

Nationwide. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is Eden legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
