Does MEDVi take insurance?

MEDVi is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do MEDVi visits work?

Async, ~24h provider review. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.
