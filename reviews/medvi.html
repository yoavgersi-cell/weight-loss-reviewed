MEDVi FAQ

How much does MEDVi cost?

MEDVi starts at $179/mo — all-in, includes medication. On 12-month prepay; month-to-month is $299/mo; tirzepatide higher. Confirm the current price at health.usnews.com.

What medications does MEDVi prescribe?

Compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with MEDVi.

Does MEDVi take insurance?

MEDVi is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do MEDVi visits work?

Async, ~24h provider review. You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is MEDVi available?

All 50 states. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is MEDVi legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
