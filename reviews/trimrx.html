How TrimRx works

Getting started with TrimRx follows the standard telehealth path: you fill in an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it (async questionnaire (limited coaching)), and if a GLP-1 is appropriate, semaglutide & tirzepatide is prescribed and shipped to your door. It includes medication, consult, supplies, shipping, monitoring.

  1. Medical intake — a short online questionnaire about your health, history and goals.

TrimRx FAQ

How much does TrimRx cost?

TrimRx starts at $179/mo — all-in, includes medication. Advertised 'from $179/mo'; semaglutide ~$199–$349/mo by plan length; branded far higher. Confirm the current price at health.usnews.com.

What medications does TrimRx prescribe?

Compounded + branded options, taken as weekly injection. Availability of any specific product shifts with supply, so check current options with TrimRx.

Does TrimRx take insurance?

TrimRx is cash-pay. Insurance rarely covers compounded GLP-1s, so members pay out of pocket, though HSA/FSA funds usually work.

How do TrimRx visits work?

Async questionnaire (limited coaching). You complete an online medical intake, a licensed clinician reviews it, and — if appropriate — medication ships to your door.

Where is TrimRx available?

Most US states. Confirm your state is covered before you start.

Is TrimRx legit?

Yes — a licensed clinician reviews your medical intake before any prescription is issued. Compounded GLP-1 medications aren't FDA-approved finished products, so confirm the provider's credentials and your state's availability before you sign up.
