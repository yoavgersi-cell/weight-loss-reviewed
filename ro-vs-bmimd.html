Ro vs bmiMD

Two premium programs, two different philosophies: a polished national platform versus a physician-led medical practice. Both sit at the higher end on price.

Ro and bmiMD both rank among the 12 online weight-loss programs we've scored, and they draw from the same pool of GLP-1 medications — so on the surface they can look interchangeable. They aren't. Ro is a well-known national telehealth company that offers a structured weight-management program including GLP-1 medications.. bmiMD takes a physician-led, medical-first approach to weight management.. Below we break the matchup down category by category, then tell you which one fits which kind of person.

Bottom line: Ro wins

Ro is the better pick for a smooth, scaled, brand-backed experience. bmiMD is the better pick if medical depth is the point and you'd trade some polish for a physician's close involvement. Same rough price band — the deciding factor is whether you value logistics or clinical thoroughness more.

Common questions

Is Ro better than bmiMD?

In our scoring, Ro comes out ahead — 9.1/10 to 8.2/10, winning 6 of the six categories we rate. That makes it our pick for most people. bmiMD is still the better choice if you want maximum medical oversight for a complex situation.

Which is cheaper, Ro or bmiMD?

Neither is dramatically cheaper than the other. Ro and bmiMD both sit in the premium range ($$$), so price isn't the deciding factor between them. Whichever you lean toward, compare the total cost at your maintenance dose — not just the introductory price.

Can I switch between Ro and bmiMD?

Generally, yes. Neither program locks you into a long contract, so if you start with one and it isn't the right fit you can move to the other. Just never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
