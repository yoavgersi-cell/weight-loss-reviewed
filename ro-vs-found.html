Ro vs Found

An established national brand versus the category's best coaching experience. Both are polished; they emphasize different things.

Ro and Found both rank among the 12 online weight-loss programs we've scored, and they draw from the same pool of GLP-1 medications — so on the surface they can look interchangeable. They aren't. Ro is a well-known national telehealth company that offers a structured weight-management program including GLP-1 medications.. Found pairs GLP-1 medication (where appropriate) with a genuine behavior-change program — coaching, an active community, and one of the best apps we tested.. Below we break the matchup down category by category, then tell you which one fits which kind of person.

Bottom line: Ro wins

Ro takes it narrowly on the strength of its scale, logistics and brand trust. Found is right behind and wins outright if coaching and its app are your priority — this one comes down to whether you value infrastructure or behavior support more.

The clearest daylight between them shows up in medication access, where Ro leads 9.3 to 8.8. Zoom out and the split is simple: Ro is our pick for most established brand, while Found earns Found's place as best for coaching.

On clinical support, value, onboarding, and transparency, there's little to separate them — both land within a couple of tenths, so those categories won't decide your choice.

Common questions

Is Ro better than Found?

In our scoring, Ro comes out ahead — 9.1/10 to 9.0/10, winning 3 of the six categories we rate. That makes it our pick for most people. Found is still the better choice if you want the strongest coaching and app, and will trade a little brand scale for it.

Which is cheaper, Ro or Found?

Found is the cheaper option. Found is the more budget-friendly of the two ($$, mid-range), while Ro sits higher ($$$, premium). Whichever you lean toward, compare the total cost at your maintenance dose — not just the introductory price.

Can I switch between Ro and Found?

Generally, yes. Neither program locks you into a long contract, so if you start with one and it isn't the right fit you can move to the other. Just never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
