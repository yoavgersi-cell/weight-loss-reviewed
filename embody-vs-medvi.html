Embody vs Medvi

The most complete full-service program versus a lean, no-nonsense route to a GLP-1. Support and flexibility against speed and simplicity.

Embody and Medvi both rank among the 12 online weight-loss programs we've scored, and they draw from the same pool of GLP-1 medications — so on the surface they can look interchangeable. They aren't. A full-service telehealth weight-loss program built around GLP-1 medications, live clinician visits, and ongoing coaching.. Medvi is a straightforward telehealth program offering both semaglutide and tirzepatide with a quick, uncomplicated process.. Below we break the matchup down category by category, then tell you which one fits which kind of person.

Bottom line: Embody wins

Embody is the better program for most people — more clinician time, coaching and medication flexibility. Medvi is the pick if you already know what you want and would rather skip the extras for a faster, simpler start.

Common questions

Is Embody better than Medvi?

In our scoring, Embody comes out ahead — 9.4/10 to 8.5/10, winning 6 of the six categories we rate. That makes it our pick for most people. Medvi is still the better choice if you want a quick, straightforward path to semaglutide or tirzepatide.

Which is cheaper, Embody or Medvi?

Neither is dramatically cheaper than the other. Embody and Medvi both sit in the mid-range range ($$), so price isn't the deciding factor between them. Whichever you lean toward, compare the total cost at your maintenance dose — not just the introductory price.

Can I switch between Embody and Medvi?

Generally, yes. Neither program locks you into a long contract, so if you start with one and it isn't the right fit you can move to the other. Just never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
