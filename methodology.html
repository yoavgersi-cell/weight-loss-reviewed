Editorial standards

How we score weight-loss programs

Our rankings are opinions, but they're not arbitrary. Here's exactly what we measure and how we keep it honest.

What we score

Every program gets an overall score out of 10, built from six sub-scores. We weight support and access most heavily, because they're what actually determine whether a program works for real people over time.

FactorWhat it captures
Clinical supportDo you get real clinician access and coaching that lasts beyond signup?
Medication accessRange of GLP-1 options and how easily the plan adapts to supply and budget.
ValueTotal cost for what you get — judged against comparable programs, not in a vacuum.
TransparencyIs pricing and medication information clear before you pay?
OnboardingHow fast and painless it is to get started, without cutting clinical corners.
App & trackingQuality of the tools you use day to day.

How we keep it independent

We're reader-supported: when you sign up through our links we may earn a commission. That funds the site, but it does not buy a better score or a higher ranking. Commissions do not factor into our scoring at all — a program we earn nothing from can outrank one we do, and sometimes does.

About pricing tiers

Because prices and promotions change constantly — and vary by dose and pharmacy — we show a relative tier ($, $$, $$$) rather than a fixed number that would be out of date within weeks. For current pricing, always check the provider directly through the "check offer" links.

What we don't do

  • We don't give medical advice or compare the clinical efficacy of medications.
  • We don't publish fabricated prices or invent facts about a provider.
  • We don't let a commission change a score. Ever.

Spotted something wrong?

Programs change. If a detail is out of date or we've got something wrong, tell us at hello@weightlossreviewed.com and we'll review it.
