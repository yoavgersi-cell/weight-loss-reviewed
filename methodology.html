Editorial standards

How we score weight-loss programs

Our rankings are opinions, but they're not arbitrary. Here's exactly what we measure, how we weight it, and how we keep it honest.

The four factors

Every program gets a 0–10 on each of four factors, scored from the real, sourced data we collect — starting price, medications, visit type, support, availability and track record. The overall score is the weighted average below.

FactorWeightWhat it captures
Value15%What you get for the price — not just the lowest sticker. A premium program that includes FDA-approved medication, insurance help and real support can be good value.
Support30%Real clinician access, coaching and follow-up — video visits and a care team score higher than async-only.
Medications25%Medication options and legitimacy — FDA-approved branded (Wegovy, Zepbound) rates above compounded-only, and breadth and flexibility add points.
Transparency30%How clearly and publicly pricing, terms and availability are stated — plus track record and any regulatory red flags.

Why we weight it this way

These are prescription medications, not a subscription box. So we deliberately weight support and transparency — real clinician access and a clear, honest, well-established operation — above the raw sticker price. The cheapest program isn't automatically the best: a rock-bottom compounded plan with thin oversight can score below a pricier, FDA-approved, well-supported one. We also credit FDA-approved branded medication over compounded-only, because it carries approval and consistency that compounding does not.

How we keep it independent

We're reader-supported: when you sign up through our links we may earn a commission. That funds the site, but it does not buy a better score or a higher ranking. Commissions do not factor into our scoring at all — a program we earn nothing from can outrank one we do, and sometimes does.

About the prices

Every price on the site is a real, dated figure with a source — the lowest advertised starting rate as of the date shown. Many programs quote those rates only on a prepaid multi-month or promotional plan, and membership programs bill medication separately, so month-to-month and maintenance-dose costs run higher. We say so, and we tell you to confirm the current number at the provider before you buy.

What we don't do

  • We don't give medical advice or compare the clinical efficacy of medications.
  • We don't publish fabricated prices or invent facts about a provider.
  • We don't let a commission change a score. Ever.

Spotted something wrong?

Programs change. If a detail is out of date or we've got something wrong, tell us at hello@weightlossreviewed.com and we'll review it.
