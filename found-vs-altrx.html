Common questions

Is Found better than AltRx?

In our scoring Found comes out ahead — 8.7/10 to 7.6/10, leading on transparency. It's our pick for most people; AltRx is the better choice if you want the lowest entry price and a fast start.

Which is cheaper, Found or AltRx?

AltRx has the lower advertised starting price — $89/mo vs $198/mo for Found. But read the fine print: Found is membership + medication (billed separately) and AltRx is all-in, includes medication, and the cheapest rates often need a prepaid multi-month plan — so compare the real all-in cost at your maintenance dose, not the headline.

Do Found and AltRx prescribe the same medications?

Found offers compounded + branded (wegovy, zepbound) (injection or oral); AltRx offers compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide (weekly injection). On breadth of options, Found scores higher. Compounded GLP-1s aren't FDA-approved finished products, and availability shifts — confirm current options with each provider.

Which gives you more support, Found or AltRx?

Found scores higher on support — real clinician access, coaching and follow-up. For visits, Found is video (insurance) or async (self-pay) and AltRx is async, video when needed.

Are Found and AltRx legit?

Both require a licensed clinician to review your intake before prescribing. Confirm each provider's credentials, state availability (Found: nationwide; AltRx: not stated) and current pricing before you sign up — and note that compounded medications aren't FDA-approved.

Can I switch between Found and AltRx?

Generally yes — neither locks you into a long contract, though the lowest prices often require a prepaid plan. Never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
