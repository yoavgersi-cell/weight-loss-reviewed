How Much Does Compounded Semaglutide Cost Online?

By the Weight Loss Reviewed team·September 8, 2026

Compounded semaglutide is usually the cheaper route — but 'cheaper' hides a wide range. Here's what actually moves the number.

In this guide
  1. What "compounded" means
  2. What drives the price
  3. How to compare offers fairly

Compounded semaglutide is often marketed as the budget-friendly way onto a GLP-1, and broadly that's true — it's typically cheaper than the branded injectables. But the quotes you'll see online swing widely, and the reasons aren't always obvious from the landing page. Here's what actually drives the number so you can compare offers on equal terms.

What "compounded" means

Compounded medications are prepared by a pharmacy rather than sold as a mass-produced branded product. That can lower cost, but it also means quality and sourcing vary by pharmacy — so who prepares your medication matters as much as the price. A responsible program is transparent about its pharmacy partners.

What drives the price

  • Your dose. Semaglutide is titrated upward over time. A low starting dose is cheaper than a maintenance dose, so an intro price may not reflect what you'll pay in a few months.
  • Membership vs. medication. Some programs split the bill into a membership fee plus medication; others bundle it. Bundled looks simpler; split can be cheaper — read both carefully.
  • Visit and lab fees. An initial consult or lab work may be extra, or may be included. This is where "from $X" quotes quietly diverge.
  • Plan length. Quarterly or longer commitments often carry a lower monthly rate than month-to-month, at the cost of flexibility.

How to compare offers fairly

Put every offer into the same shape before you judge it: total cost for a full month at your expected maintenance dose, including membership, visits, and shipping. A program that's cheapest at the starter dose isn't necessarily cheapest where you'll actually spend most of your time. This is exactly why our reviews score value and transparency as separate things.

A note on very low prices

If a quote is far below everyone else, find out why before you celebrate. Ask about the pharmacy, what's included, and what happens at higher doses. Unusually cheap sometimes means something's missing.
