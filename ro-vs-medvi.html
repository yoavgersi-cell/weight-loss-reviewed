Common questions

Is Ro better than Medvi?

In our scoring Ro comes out ahead — 9.1/10 to 8.2/10, leading on transparency. It's our pick for most people; Medvi is the better choice if you want a simpler, faster route and care less about the brand.

Which is cheaper, Ro or Medvi?

Ro has the lower advertised starting price — $39/mo vs $179/mo for Medvi. But read the fine print: Ro is membership + medication (billed separately) and Medvi is all-in, includes medication, and the cheapest rates often need a prepaid multi-month plan — so compare the real all-in cost at your maintenance dose, not the headline.

Do Ro and Medvi prescribe the same medications?

Ro offers wegovy, zepbound, ozempic & foundaya (oral pill or injection pen); Medvi offers compounded semaglutide & tirzepatide (weekly injection). On breadth of options, Ro scores higher. Compounded GLP-1s aren't FDA-approved finished products, and availability shifts — confirm current options with each provider.

Which gives you more support, Ro or Medvi?

Ro scores higher on support — real clinician access, coaching and follow-up. For visits, Ro is async + optional video and Medvi is async, ~24h provider review.

Are Ro and Medvi legit?

Both require a licensed clinician to review your intake before prescribing. Confirm each provider's credentials, state availability (Ro: nationwide; Medvi: all 50 states) and current pricing before you sign up — and note that compounded medications aren't FDA-approved.

Can I switch between Ro and Medvi?

Generally yes — neither locks you into a long contract, though the lowest prices often require a prepaid plan. Never stop or change a GLP-1 medication without talking to your clinician first.
